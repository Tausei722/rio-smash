Use of the Microphone

This app requests microphone access for the pronunciation practice feature. Microphone input is used only for speech recognition and score calculation and is not stored as a recording on our servers.

Data Storage and Protection

Account information is stored on the Supabase platform and transmitted over encrypted connections. We handle the information only to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes stated above.

Data Deletion / Account Deletion

Users can delete their account from the "My Page" screen in the app. Deleting the account removes the account information and associated usage data that we hold. For details, see the account deletion instructions page or contact us by email.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by parents and children together. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Please use the app under the supervision of a parent or guardian.
